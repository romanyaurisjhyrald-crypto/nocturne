# Visual direction and references

Research was restricted to Diego Perera / Not Perera, using his official portfolio and linked profiles:

- https://notperera.artstation.com/
- https://notperera.artstation.com/projects/wryzl6 — Speed Devil concept trailer. The artist describes Crazy Taxi and Jet Set Radio as influences.
- https://notperera.artstation.com/projects/a0dBJJ — CRIME expression sheet.
- https://linktr.ee/notperera — official profile links.
- https://bsky.app/profile/notperera.bsky.social — Speed Devils banner reference.

The banner's warm/cool contrast, energetic graphic composition and expressive character presentation informed the direction. This is an original UI interpretation: cream reading surfaces, halftone decoration, coral slashes, angular multicolored buttons, outlined typography and hand-drawn-style starbursts. It does not reproduce the artist's illustrations, logos or compositions. Source artwork was not downloaded into the game.

The approved user-provided mouse remains the character. Its model, eyes and existing rig were not redesigned to match a portfolio character. Readability and control hit areas take priority over decorative marks.
