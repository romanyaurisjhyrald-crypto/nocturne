# Space Apps 2026 submission record

Project: Nocturne. Team: Nebula Drift. Local event supplied by creator: Andahuaylas, Peru.

Challenge: Abandoned but not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars.

The creator-supplied brief asks for stories introducing school-age enthusiasts to NASA hardware on the Moon, Mars and in deep space and to the science it made possible. Nocturne addresses this through lunar laser ranging, Opportunity exploration, Voyager travel and heritage discovery.
